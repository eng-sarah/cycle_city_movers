How to play

  1. Click any box to pick up its crate.
  2. The home box will glow pulsing green.
  3. Click ANY box to swap. (Try to place it in the correct home to save moves!)
  4. Complete cycles to sort the city.

Goal: Sort with minimal writes. (N - cycles).

Strategy

You can swap anywhere, but random swaps usually waste moves. Always try to place the carried crate into its Home.

Scoring

Finish near optimal to get 3★. Extra writes reduce stars.
